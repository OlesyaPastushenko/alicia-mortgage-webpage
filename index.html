A FEW THINGS YOU MAY BE WONDERING

Questions welcome.
Always.

Do I need to have found a property already?

You can start a conversation while you are still exploring. Understanding your budget and the information lenders may need can help you plan your search.

Can we talk in English?

Yes. You can speak with me in English, Spanish, Ukrainian or Russian.

What should I prepare for our first conversation?

A rough property budget, whether the home is for holidays or a move, and a broad picture of your employment and finances. Please don’t send identity documents or bank statements through this initial form.

Will I understand the costs before I commit?

We will discuss the scope of support and any applicable service fees before you proceed. You should also understand the lender’s conditions and the purchase costs before making a commitment.

Is a mortgage approval guaranteed?

No. Approval and terms depend on the lender’s assessment of your circumstances and the property. Personal guidance helps you understand the process; the lending decision remains with the bank.
